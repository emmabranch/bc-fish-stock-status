Management context

Text in quotation marks is copied exactly as published in DFO’s 2024 Sustainability Survey for Fisheries, including any typos.

  • Limit reference point, as described by DFO: “0.4 of BMSY, where BMSY is 22,720 metric tonnes Estimated reference points in 2022 available from: https://www.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/csas-sccs/Publications/SAR-AS/2023/2023_010-eng.html”
  • Upper stock reference, as described by DFO: “0.8 of BMSY, where BMSY is 22,720 metric tonnes. Estimated reference points in 2022 available from: https://www.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/csas-sccs/Publications/SAR-AS/2023/2023_010-eng.html”
  • Limit used for this project’s indicator: 0.4 x Bmsy (DFO default proxy; the 2024 survey reports this LRP for sablefish)
  • Management plan, as reported by DFO: “Pacific Integrated Fisheries Management Plan - GROUNDFISH - Effective Feb 21, 2024 - Feb 20, 2025 (Current Pacific Integrated Fisheries Management plan - GROUNDFISH - Effective Feb 21, 2025 - Feb 20, 2026)”
  • Managed by: Fisheries and Oceans Canada (DFO)
