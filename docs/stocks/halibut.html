Management context

Text in quotation marks is copied exactly as published in DFO’s 2024 Sustainability Survey for Fisheries, including any typos.

  • Limit reference point, as described by DFO: “LRP set at 0.2 of the equilibrium un-fished female spawning biomass (SB20). At the end of 2023, SB was estimated to be 174,000,000 net lbs. From this, SB20 = 34,800,000 net lbs.”
  • Upper stock reference, as described by DFO: “USR set at 0.3 of the equilibrium un-fished female spawning biomass (SB30). At the end of 2023, SB was estimated to be 174,000,000 net lbs. From this, SB30 = 52,200,000 net lbs.”
  • Limit used for this project’s indicator: SSBlim from RAM (IPHC limit; the 2024 survey describes the LRP as 0.2 x unfished female spawning biomass)
  • Management plan, as reported by DFO: “Pacific Integrated Fisheries Management Plan - GROUNDFISH - Effective Feb 21, 2024 - Feb 20, 2025 (Current Pacific Integrated Fisheries Management plan - GROUNDFISH - Effective Feb 21, 2025 - Feb 20, 2026)”
  • Managed by: DFO, co-managed with United States of America via the International Pacific Halibut Commission

Data gaps and caveats

  • Time series ends in 2020; the DFO zone is from the 2024 survey.
  • Fishing-pressure ratio available only to 2013.
  • Limit reference point taken or derived from RAM Legacy; DFO’s current value may differ.
